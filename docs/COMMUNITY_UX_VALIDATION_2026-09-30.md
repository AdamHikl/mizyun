# COMM-01 adult target-user validation pack

**Issue:** [#140](https://github.com/AdamHikl/mizyun/issues/140). **Prototype:** [Community contract, revision 2026-09-30](COMMUNITY_PRODUCT_CONTRACT.md). **Evidence status:** Prepared; no participant sessions performed or results inferred. Text-wireframe comprehension is design evidence, not functioning-app/device acceptance under #150/#155.

## Purpose and recruiting

Test whether people understand deliberate sharing and feel free to decline. Use 3–5 adult target users, including at least one person who voluntarily declines Community. Seek people who track workouts and would encourage an existing friend; include a participant using large text or assistive technology where feasible. If nobody declines, recruit someone interested in private tracking who prefers not to join; never pressure or coach them into refusal. Do not infer adult eligibility from appearance.

The founder/user conducts voluntary research; this document does not authorize the agent to message or recruit anyone. Ask for separate study consent; refusal has no effect on private app access. Use anonymous IDs P01–P05, an adult declaration only, no full DOB, health logs, real names, real handles or contact details in repository/GitHub notes. Avoid audio/video/screenshots containing personal information. Use fictional workouts and profiles. Participant contact/recruitment data stays outside repository notes and is deleted when recruitment ends; raw anonymous session notes are retained 30 days after synthesis, then only anonymized findings/decisions remain. Do not promise anonymity if the founder can identify participants from their context.

## Moderator script and tasks

Say: “This is a proposed design. No account or workout will be shared. You can stop, decline Community or skip questions. We are testing whether the design is clear, not testing you. Please describe what you expect before I explain anything.”

Show the contract's wireframes A–H one at a time at a narrow-screen size. First ask without prompting; record observed interpretation before any explanation. Use these scenarios:

| Task                                                       | Critical comprehension / observable success                                                                                                                                             |
| ---------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A: choose whether to join or decline                       | Private tracking/Coach remain available; no prechecked consent/discovery; participant can find “Not now” without pressure. Record real voluntary join/decline preference.               |
| A: review missing year / boundary-year rejection           | Understand that self-reported year is not exact age verification; a boundary-year adult may wait; no assumption that ID is required or AI permission grants entry.                      |
| B: find a friend with search off / receive invite          | Exact search needs separate permission; bearer invite can identify inviter to an eligible signed-in recipient; possession never automatically follows or approves.                      |
| C: approve a follower, then consider following back        | Directional permission; new follower sees existing still-shared history and future cards; following back is separate. Own graph is private.                                             |
| D: save workout and preview sharing                        | Saving stays private; generic category and chosen day only, duration initially off; no names/sets/health notes; future approved audience and copies already seen understood.            |
| D/E: unshare, edit private workout, remove follower        | Unshare leaves private workout; ordinary private edit doesn't update snapshot; removing one follower removes only that person's access/reactions; screenshot recall cannot be promised. |
| C/F: mute, block, report and appeal                        | Mute affects feed/push; block ends both edges; unblocking restores nothing; reports confidential to staff, no automatic punishment, emergency services for imminent danger.             |
| F/H: content disappears, connection fails, user restricted | Report recent abuse through restricted safety route/contact without restored browsing; no “sent” receipt offline; private tracking remains usable.                                      |
| G/H: withdraw, return, switch accounts or lose connection  | Old graph/cards/invites do not return; blocks persist; no stale social browsing; server withdrawal must be confirmed; limited retention differs from immediate access denial.           |

Finish with: “What made you hesitate? What would you change? Would you use this, decline it or decide later? Which information would you never want to share?” Record preference and reasons without marketing or collecting actual health details. Ask declined participants whether continuing privately feels equally supported.

## Acceptance and correction loop

Before #140 closure, obtain 3–5 genuine sessions including a voluntary decliner. Every participant must explain critical boundaries: optional participation; separate discovery and other permissions; directional approval with existing/future history; minimal deliberate publication; withdrawal/block behavior and limits of recalling seen content. Record first-attempt misunderstandings rather than erasing them after coaching. Any material misunderstanding needs a named copy/layout change, issue owner and fresh retest; final critical comprehension must pass for all participants. If it does not, keep design acceptance open and revise. Preferences against the feature are valid findings, not comprehension failures.

Assistive-device interaction, native QR/app links, latency/retry behavior, actual server permission denial and real report delivery are explicitly untested by paper wireframes. Carry these to #144/#150/#152/#155 rather than checking them passed. Prototype absence or missing staffing is not blamed on participants.
