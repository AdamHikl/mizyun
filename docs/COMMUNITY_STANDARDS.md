## 1. What Community is

Community is an optional way for adults to encourage each other. Members can follow each other with approval, share workouts, meals and indoor bouldering sessions, and react to what their friends share. Mizyun may add further optional features later, such as comments, direct messages, spaces (groups), challenges, routine sharing, photos and broader search. They are not part of Community unless and until Mizyun enables them in the app; where enabled, these Standards apply to them too.

- **Nothing is shared automatically.** Saving a workout, meal or climb keeps it private. You preview and confirm each item before it is shared, and the preview shows who will see it. Optional details, such as nutrition or completed sets, start switched off.
- **Adults only (18+).** Joining requires an adult declaration, acceptance of the in-app Community rules, and a conservative check of the birth year in your profile: a birth year at least 19 years before the current UTC year passes. The check relies on what you enter and does not verify identity or age.
- **Private tracking is separate.** Declining Community, leaving it, or receiving a moderation decision does not affect private tracking, Coach access or paid access. Community has no access to your private health integrations or Coach context. Meal notes, the photos kept with your meals, and workout titles and notes stay private.
- **Separate choices.** Joining enables none of the optional features automatically. If enabled, search, messages, spaces, challenges, photos and routine sharing each have their own choice, and messages require mutual following and both people to opt in. There is no public feed and no contact upload.
- **Leaving and returning.** Leaving immediately hides your profile and shared content, ends follows and pending requests, and revokes invitations. Returning requires fresh choices and restores no earlier follows, posts, invitations or reactions. Blocks and moderation restrictions remain.

## 2. What is not allowed

These rules apply to everything in Community: handles, display names, bios, shared workouts, meals and climbs, reactions, and, if those features are enabled, photos, comments, messages, routines, and posts in spaces and challenges.

### 2.1 Harassment and bullying

- Insults, threats, intimidation, trolling or persistent unwanted contact.
- Sending follow requests or messages again after being declined, removed or blocked.
- Getting around a block with another account or handle.
- Retaliatory reporting or coordinated abuse of a member.

### 2.2 Hate

Content that attacks, dehumanizes or incites hatred against people because of race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, disability, medical condition, age, sex, gender identity or sexual orientation.

### 2.3 Body shaming

Mocking or degrading anyone's weight, body or appearance, ridiculing their training capacity, weights or pace, or giving unsolicited critiques of their physique.

### 2.4 Dangerous fitness and diet practices

- Promoting or glorifying eating disorders, starvation or extreme fasting.
- Encouraging people to train through serious injury or warning signs, or beyond safe limits.
- Promoting illicit performance-enhancing drugs, steroids or unprescribed medication.
- Self-harm presented as discipline.

### 2.5 Scams and commercial solicitation

Community is for encouragement between members, not for selling. Do not post unsolicited advertising, client or affiliate solicitation, deceptive health, supplement or financial claims, or requests for money or gifts.

### 2.6 Spam and inauthentic behavior

Automated or repetitive posting, requests or reactions; meaningless or flooding text; manipulating reactions or challenge results.

### 2.7 Impersonation

Do not pose as another person, falsely claim to be a trainer, dietitian or medical professional, or claim to represent Mizyun. Handles such as `admin`, `moderator`, `official`, `staff`, `support` and `mizyun` are reserved.

### 2.8 Sexual content and child sexual exploitation

- **Child sexual exploitation and abuse is never allowed.** This includes any content that depicts, promotes or facilitates the sexual exploitation, abuse or grooming of a child. If you see it, do not share or save it; report it in the app and write to adoh811@gmail.com. Mizyun treats these reports as urgent, removes the content, restricts the account, and reports to the competent authorities where the law requires.
- **No sexual content.** Sexually explicit images or text, sexual solicitation, pornography and sexually suggestive handles or names are not allowed.

### 2.9 Illegal and dangerous activity

Selling or promoting illegal drugs or weapons, instructions for making dangerous devices, credible threats of violence, terrorism, or other content that facilitates illegal acts.

### 2.10 Private and health information

- Do not disclose another person's health information, diagnoses, medication or fitness records, or their contact details, address or real name, without their consent.
- Keep sensitive health details, links, email addresses and phone numbers out of social text. Bios do not accept links, email addresses or phone numbers.

## 3. Your safety tools

- **Discovery is your choice.** Search is off by default. With search off, a valid invitation you share can still let someone ask to follow you.
- **Approval to follow.** Nobody can follow you without your approval. Approving a follower does not follow them back.
- **Block.** Blocking hides both of you from each other, ends follows in both directions, and stops interaction between you.
- **Mute.** Muting hides a member's posts from you without notifying them or changing who can see your posts.
- **Report.** You can report a profile or shared post and, if those features are enabled, a comment, message, space, space post, challenge, routine or photo, choosing harassment, offensive content, impersonation, spam, unsafe or sexual content, or other, with up to 1,000 characters of details. When you report a profile you can also block it. You can report a recent interaction for up to 30 days, even after the content has gone. Reports accept no attachments.
- **Confidential reports.** The app does not show the reported member who reported them. Only authorized moderators can see reports. Report details or circumstances may still let someone guess who reported them, and disclosure required by law is handled under the applicable law.
- **Limits.** Each member can open up to 10 new report cases per day. The safety contact remains available when that limit is reached.
- **Help & safety.** In the app, Community > Help & safety shows your reports and their status, any moderation decisions about you, appeals, and the safety contact.

## 4. How Mizyun moderates

Mizyun automatically filters handles, display names and bios before they appear. Automated filters are incomplete, so reports matter. If photo sharing is enabled, a photo is held until it has been reviewed and is not shown to anyone else before then, so it may stay pending. Reports are reviewed by Mizyun's moderator, currently one person, the founder. Moderators see only the reported Community content and report details, never private workouts, meals, photos or Coach data. Community content is not sent to an AI provider for moderation. Filing a report does not by itself lead to any action.

**Review targets.** Mizyun aims to review reports within **24 hours** of receipt, and reports of harassment or unsafe or sexual content within **4 hours**. With a single moderator these are targets, not guarantees, and Mizyun is not an emergency service.

Mizyun chooses a proportionate action based on severity and prior record:

| Action              | What happens                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| :------------------ | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Warning**         | You receive a notice in Help & safety explaining the decision. Nothing else changes.                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Content removal** | The reported content is removed. For a profile, its display name and bio are cleared and the profile is hidden; you can rejoin with fresh choices.                                                                                         |
| **Suspension**      | Your Community access is suspended for 7, 14 or 30 days. Your profile and shared content are hidden and your connections end. After the suspension ends you can rejoin with fresh choices; earlier posts and connections are not restored. |
| **Ban**             | You are permanently removed from Community and cannot rejoin while the account exists.                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Urgent cases**    | Reports of harassment, threats, and unsafe or sexual content, including child-safety concerns, are reviewed first and handled with the actions above; further steps follow the applicable law.                                             |

During a suspension or ban you keep private tracking, Coach access, block management, appeals, the safety contact, export, deletion and leaving Community. A separate, serious or repeated breach of the Terms of Service can also lead Mizyun to restrict the whole account.

## 5. Appeals

You can appeal a warning, removal, suspension or ban once, in Community > Help & safety, with up to 1,000 characters of explanation.

- An appeal is decided by a moderator who did not make the original decision, and never by the person who reported you.
- Mizyun currently has a single moderator. While that is the case, an appeal stays open until a second moderator is available to decide it, and the original decision stays in effect meanwhile.
- If the appeal succeeds, the restriction from that decision is lifted. Removed posts and ended connections are not restored automatically, and returning to Community needs fresh choices. Any other active restriction stays in place.
- You can check the appeal's status in Help & safety.
- Appealing never leads to a harsher decision.

You can also write to adoh811@gmail.com about a decision or an open appeal.
