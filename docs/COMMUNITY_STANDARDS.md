## 1. Purpose, Scope, and Guiding Principles

Mizyun Community is an optional social experience designed for consenting adult friends to encourage each other through deliberately published workout completion cards. Mizyun's core product is private, trustworthy fitness and nutrition tracking. Participation in Community is strictly voluntary, opt-in, and auxiliary.

### Core Principles

- **Deliberate Sharing Only:** Saving a workout never automatically publishes it. Users preview and confirm every card shared with their audience.
- **Adult Participation (18+):** Community enrollment requires an explicit adult confirmation and conservative server-side age eligibility verification (`birthYear <= currentUtcYear - 19`).
- **Complete Decoupling from Private Health:** Declining to join Community, withdrawing at any time, or incurring social moderation sanctions has **zero effect** on private workout logging, food tracking, body measurements, AI Coach access, or paid subscription entitlements.
- **Mutual Respect & Positive Encouragement:** Mizyun is built for genuine peer encouragement. It is not an algorithmic popularity contest, public broadcast feed, or marketplace.

## 2. Prohibited Content and Conduct

All user-generated text—including account handles, display names, profile bios, and shared activity snapshots—must adhere to these Community Standards. Mizyun enforces zero tolerance for malicious, exploitative, or harmful activity.

### 2.1 Harassment and Bullying

We prohibit behavior intended to demean, intimidate, humiliate, or distress another person. This includes:

- Targeted insults, persistent unwanted contact, or trolling.
- Repeatedly sending follow requests after being declined or canceled.
- Attempting to circumvent user blocks or mutes through alternative handles or accounts.
- Retaliatory reporting or coordinated abuse campaigns against any member.

### 2.2 Hate Speech and Discriminatory Conduct

We strictly prohibit any content or conduct that attacks, demeans, incites hatred against, or dehumanizes individuals or groups based on protected characteristics, including:

- Race, ethnicity, national origin, or citizenship.
- Religion, faith, or spiritual beliefs.
- Physical disability, neurodivergence, or medical conditions.
- Age, sex, gender identity, or sexual orientation.

### 2.3 Body Shaming and Appearance Degradation

Mizyun exists to support healthy personal progress, not toxic aesthetic comparison. We prohibit:

- Mocking, degrading, or criticizing any person's weight, body fat, muscle definition, or physical appearance.
- Negative commentary on a user's workout capacity, training weights, or pace.
- Unsolicited critiques of another person's physique.

### 2.4 Dangerous Fitness Practices and Extreme Diets

To protect user physical and mental well-being, we prohibit:

- Content promoting, glorifying, or encouraging eating disorders (including anorexia nervosa, bulimia, pro-ana, or pro-mia content).
- Promotion of severe, medically unsound starvation or extreme water-fasting regimens.
- Encouraging individuals to train through acute traumatic injuries, cardiac distress, or beyond safe physiological limits.
- Promotion, distribution, or glorification of illicit performance-enhancing drugs, anabolic-androgenic steroids (AAS), or unprescribed pharmaceuticals.
- Self-harm masked as fitness discipline.

### 2.5 Scams, Fraud, and Commercial Solicitation

Community is dedicated to personal friendships, not commercial advertising. Prohibited activities include:

- Unsolicited commercial promotions, personal training client solicitation, or affiliate marketing.
- Deceptive health, supplement, or financial claims ("quick-fix" weight loss pills, pyramid schemes, or crypto promotion).
- Seeking monetary contributions, gifts, or financial transactions.

### 2.6 Spam and Inauthentic Behavior

We prohibit:

- Automated, programmatic, or repetitive submissions of handles, requests, or activity cards.
- Meaningless characters, gibberish bios, or repetitive text flooding queues or feeds.
- Deceptive metrics or reaction manipulation.

### 2.7 Impersonation and Deceptive Identity

You must represent yourself truthfully. Prohibited actions include:

- Posing as another real individual without explicit authorization.
- Falsely claiming to be a certified personal trainer, registered dietitian, medical professional, or clinical authority.
- Claiming false affiliation with Mizyun, including attempting to register reserved system handles (`admin`, `staff`, `moderator`, `official`, `mizyun`, `support`, etc.).

### 2.8 Sexual Content and Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAE/CSAM)

- **Child Exploitation & Abuse (CSAE/CSAM):** Mizyun maintains **absolute zero tolerance** for any content depicting, promoting, or facilitating child sexual exploitation, abuse, grooming, or harm. Such violations result in immediate permanent account termination, evidentiary preservation, and mandatory referral to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and law enforcement authorities.
- **Adult Sexual Content:** Sexually explicit bios, sexual solicitation, pornography, or sexually suggestive handles/names are strictly prohibited.

### 2.9 Illegal and Dangerous Activities

We prohibit content that facilitates or encourages illegal acts under applicable law, including:

- The sale, distribution, or promotion of illicit drugs or controlled substances.
- Weapons trafficking or instructions for manufacturing dangerous devices.
- Direct, credible threats of physical violence, terrorism, or criminal acts.

### 2.10 Private Health Information and Doxxing

Protecting privacy is central to Mizyun. We prohibit:

- Disclosing another person's private health data, medical diagnoses, medication history, or personal fitness logs without explicit consent.
- Publishing personal contact information (real names, physical addresses, phone numbers, private email addresses) of any individual in bios or handles.
- Sharing private clinical health data within public or semi-public social text fields.

## 3. Safety, Blocking, and Reporting Tools

Mizyun provides robust, user-controlled privacy and moderation tools accessible across the mobile experience:

1. **Exact-Handle Discovery Only:** Users are never placed in a public directory by default. Handle lookup is strictly opt-in and requires an exact match.
2. **Directional Approval:** Nobody can follow you without your explicit approval. Approving a follower does not automatically follow them back.
3. **Bilateral Blocking:** Blocking an individual instantly severs all relationship edges in both directions, revokes interaction privileges, and renders both profiles mutually invisible.
4. **Muting:** Muting hides an individual's shared cards from your feed without notifying them or severing your follow relationship.
5. **Confidential Reporting:**
   - Users can report profiles, shared activities, or recent abuse (within 30 days of interaction, even after blocking).
   - Reports are completely confidential. **The identity of the reporter is never revealed to the reported account under any circumstances.**
   - Rate limits prevent reporting abuse (maximum 10 new report cases per user per day).

## 4. Enforcement Tiers

When a Community Standards violation is identified—either through automated pre-publication text filtering or staffed human review—Mizyun takes fair, graduated, and proportionate enforcement action based on severity and prior record.

| Tier          | Action                       | Scope & Impact                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| :------------ | :--------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Tier 1**    | **Formal Warning**           | Issued for minor, first-time infractions (e.g., borderline bio text). The user receives an in-app notice explaining the policy. No privileges are restricted.                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Tier 2**    | **Content Removal**          | The violating content (e.g., an inappropriate bio or offending shared card) is permanently removed from social surfaces. The user's bio or display name may be reset to default placeholders.                                                                                                                                |
| **Tier 3**    | **Temporary Suspension**     | For repeated violations or moderate-severity infractions (e.g., harassment, body shaming). Community access is suspended for a fixed term (7, 14, or 30 days). The user cannot publish cards, react, or follow others. **Private tracking and AI features remain completely accessible.**                                    |
| **Tier 4**    | **Permanent Community Ban**  | For severe infractions (e.g., hate speech, severe harassment, scamming, repeat offenses). The account's Community enrollment is permanently terminated, and re-enrollment is barred across account generations. **Underlying private tracking remains available unless Mizyun Terms of Service are independently breached.** |
| **Emergency** | **Urgent restricted review** | Child-safety concerns and credible threats require prioritized social containment and the accepted escalation playbook. Any broader account action or statutory reporting is handled separately under applicable policy and law.                                                                                             |

## 5. Right of Appeal and Second Review

We recognize that misunderstandings and false positives can occur. Every user subject to a moderation decision (warning, removal, suspension or ban) has the right to a fair, objective appeal.

### 5.1 How to File an Appeal

- **In-App:** Navigate to the moderation notice in Account & Settings > Community Safety > Appeals.
- **By Email:** Send your appeal to the verified safety contact shown in the app, including your case reference ID, account email, and reason for appeal.

### 5.2 Independent Second-Review Guarantee

To ensure complete fairness, **all appeals are reviewed by an independent second operator who was not involved in the original enforcement decision**.

- The reviewing operator conducts an objective reassessment of the reported text snapshot and the user's explanation.
- If the appeal is upheld, the linked sanction is reversed and the decision is audited. Other sanctions remain effective; cards and relationships are not restored automatically. Fresh enrollment consent is required before returning to Community.
- View the appeal status in Community safety. Notification delivery is a separate acceptance dependency.
- **Filing an appeal is entirely safe:** Submitting an appeal will never result in retaliatory penalties or increased sanctions.
