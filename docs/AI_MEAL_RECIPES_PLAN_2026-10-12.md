## A. Context and scope

Help the user answer: **What can I cook with what I have that fits the balance of my remaining macros?** Meals owns a compact **What can I cook?** action; Coach opens the same screen and may explain or help adjust the result. No new top-level tab.

Adam's requirement: compare remaining protein, carbohydrate and fat in grams together. With **50 g protein, 150 g carbohydrate and 10 g fat** remaining, favor carbohydrate-rich food with lean protein and modest fat. A meal contributes toward the relevant gaps; it does not have to consume all remaining macros or calories. “Most missing” means the remaining macro balance, not simply the largest number of grams or an instruction to eat every gram today.

### First complete release scope

- Typed ingredients and optional fridge/cabinet photos, including two separate captures under the existing server-authorized multi-photo capability. A one-photo account can combine locations in one image or add further ingredients as text; do not bypass the current one/basic, up-to-three/advanced boundary.
- One short editable ingredient review after recognition; merge duplicates across images. Quantities are optional for inventory recognition and explicit in the proposed recipe.
- Two or three distinct feasible meals with ingredient quantities, servings, preparation time, ordered cooking instructions, estimated calories/P/C/F per serving, and a short deterministic explanation of macro fit.
- Explicit exclusions, optional preparation-time limit, familiar foods where available, sensible portions, and visible missing ingredients. Default to confirmed available ingredients; extra ingredients require user opt-in.
- Portion adjustment without another provider request, recipe details, and **Log what I ate** opening the existing editable meal draft. Selecting, cooking or viewing a recipe changes no consumed totals.
- Qualified logged-so-far context, honest unknown/empty/error states, owner isolation, stale-result handling, and recoverable selected-meal handoff.

Non-goals: permanent pantry inventory, expiry recognition, shopping lists, weekly menus, recipe sharing, appliance control, calorie-target changes, automatic intake or memories, clinical diets, exhaustive branded-food lookup, billing implementation, or a separate Coach recipe engine. A permanent recipe library is a later slice; pending recipe recovery is not a library.

### User journey

1. Meals → What can I cook? Show remaining P/C/F with **Based on what you've logged today**. Allow a brief “Anything else eaten?” correction through existing logging, without demanding full-day completion before lunch.
2. Enter ingredients or take/select photos. Existing AI consent applies before provider sharing. Photo recognition returns ingredient candidates; the user removes mistakes and confirms the list in one action.
3. Show up to three practical options. Example explanation: **Carbs and protein are further below your targets; this option adds both with relatively little fat.** Use this wording only when the actual snapshot supports it.
4. Open recipe, choose servings/portion, inspect estimates and cooking steps. Recalculate locally; substitutions require remapping ingredients and recalculating before maintaining a fit claim.
5. After eating, Log what I ate → existing meal form → review actual consumed serving/name/macros → Save. Leave an existing unsaved meal draft intact until the user explicitly resolves the collision.

### Data and authority

The API derives owner identity from authentication and recomputes the current local-day totals and energy targets from owned records. Client remaining grams are display hints, never server authority. Exhaust all required meal pages; capped, stale or failed reads cannot produce a confident fit result. Keep unknown nutrients distinct from a proven empty day and from known zero.

Capture local date, timezone, generated time, formula/ranking/catalog versions, goal/profile/constraint revisions and an intake revision or canonical fingerprint. Re-read relevant inputs before returning a fit result; discard/recompute if they changed. Pending results become stale on goal/intake/exclusion changes, local midnight, timezone changes and account/session transitions. Reopening an old recipe may retain archival cooking details, but never yesterday's “fits today” claim. Before offering it for cooking or recipe-derived handoff, revalidate current exclusions, diet and ingredient compatibility. Failed or unavailable validation leaves details visibly stale and disables those actions; independent manual logging of food already eaten remains available.

PC-01 whole-day completion is for retrospective inference; it cannot establish completeness of a day still in progress. For recipes distinguish **logged so far**, **user confirmed up to a timestamp**, **unknown/truncated**, and completed-day evidence. Never infer complete intake from meal count. Numeric fit is a qualified comparison to known logged values, not a statement of physiological need. Missing targets or any required macro total disable the full numeric best-fit label; ordinary ingredient-based suggestions remain available.

Provider calls retain ADR-0024 authenticated adult consent, current entitlements, server kill switches, durable limits and the approved founder/partner Dev exception. No new founder approval or email list. Source development with fake adapters can start without live-provider acceptance; new live evaluation and broader access retain [BEFORE_TESTING](BEFORE_TESTING.md) and Q7/Q8 gates.

## C. Staged implementation steps

### REC-01 — Macro context, ranking and reference cases

**Files:** [NEW] `packages/core/src/meal-recipe.ts`, `meal-recipe.test.ts`; [NEW] `packages/contracts/src/meal-recipe.ts`, `meal-recipe.test.ts`; [MODIFY] their public `index.ts` exports. Reference the existing energy/daily-summary contracts without changing target formulas. Add the recipe requirement IDs to `docs/PRODUCT_REQUIREMENTS.md` and the feature map during implementation, coordinated with their owners.

Define versioned recipe context and result contracts: nullable target/consumed/signed-remaining grams; coverage and source snapshot; confirmed ingredient IDs/names and preparation state; quantity and availability state; exclusions; preferences; ingredient weights; servings; steps; computed nutrition/ranges/provenance; fit state and reason codes. Limit lists, text, weights and response bytes. Ingredient IDs must resolve in the server-selected catalog, not become trusted because the model emitted them.

For each macro `i`, retain signed remaining `S_i = T_i - C_i` and positive gap `R_i = max(0, S_i)` only when both operands are known. Use P/C/F energy weights `e = (4,4,9)` for the ranking heuristic, without replacing the existing calorie estimate or requiring food-label energy to match 4/4/9 exactly.

For a plausible candidate portion with computed macro grams `M`, let `E_R = Σ(e_i R_i)` and `E_M = Σ(e_i M_i)`. If `E_R > 0`, set `α = min(1, E_M / E_R)` and desired meal grams `D_i = α R_i`. This preserves the missing-macro relationship at the size of a normal meal, rather than generating an entire day's remaining intake.

Initial version `macro-balance-v1` minimizes `fitLoss = Σ(e_i |M_i - D_i|) / max(E_M, 1) + 2 × Σ(e_i max(0, M_i - R_i)) / max(E_M, 1)`. The second term discourages avoidable overshoot, including a macro already over target; it is a soft ranking penalty, never a refusal to provide food. The weight 2 is a provisional product heuristic to freeze and evaluate, not a clinical rule. Candidate portion bounds and meal-size preference apply before scoring; never shrink a dish to a teaspoon or make an enormous portion to game the loss. Rank only approved normal servings and sensible scaled variants. Resolve equal fit by confirmed availability, requested prep time, estimate quality and stable recipe ID. Avoid duplicate options.

**Reference:** remaining 50 P / 150 C / 10 F gives `E_R = 890`. A 400-kcal macro-energy portion maps to approximately **22.5 P / 67.4 C / 4.5 F**, leaving approximately **27.5 P / 82.6 C / 5.5 F**. This illustrates the desired mix, not a promised recipe or mandatory serving. Acceptance must favor a feasible lean-protein/carbohydrate option over a comparable high-fat option, not merely snapshot the formula. Generate explanation focus from the actual gap and supported target context; largest raw grams alone is insufficient.

**Acceptance:** reference fixture plus protein-heavy, fat-heavy, balanced, small gaps, over-target macro, all gaps exhausted, zero target, unknown macro, missing profile, invalid numbers, raw/cooked unit mismatch and deterministic ties. No NaN, division by zero, forced compensation or “skip eating.” All gaps exhausted switches to ordinary recipe discovery. Pure/contract tests can proceed before PC-01 persistence or provider gates.
