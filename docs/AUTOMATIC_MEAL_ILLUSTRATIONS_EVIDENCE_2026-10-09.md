## Delivered behavior

Meals without retained photos immediately show a bundled16-food AI-generated catalog selected locally from English/Slovak/Czech names. Unknown meals show a neutral plate. Real retained photos retain preview/retry/removal and take priority. Saving and nutrition calculations have no dependency on artwork. There is no generation button or extra confirmation.

Unknown persisted meals may request a private server-generated icon asynchronously, when the default-off image feature is accepted and enabled. Only a bounded normalized saved dish name enters the provider request; account IDs, dates, quantities, nutrition and notes do not. Existing consent/adult checks and switches apply before dispatch and publication. Unsafe or unparseable titles remain on the fallback. The Gemini implementation uses a provider-neutral port.

Identical normalized dishes reuse an account-scoped cache while their source exists. Source deletion/name/photo changes erase its artwork; charged usage remains until account deletion. Export includes retained bytes and usage even when generation is disabled or consent is revoked. Cross-owner FK/RLS protect rows; clients cannot mutate admission. Provider failures, timeouts and restart ambiguity remain charged terminal states. Dedicated limits:5/user/UTCday,30/user/month,50/environment/day,500/environment/month; one active job per user, two per environment;120s lease and40s whole-job deadline. No provider retry.

Mobile retires requests on blur/background/offline/account/token/name changes, retries on focus/foreground/reachable-network events, and silently retains the fallback on any failure. Generated bytes are held only in the mounted view and use no native disk cache; offline remount shows the bundled catalog. Accessible labels distinguish illustrations from photos. Native visual compactness and both-platform rendering still need acceptance.
