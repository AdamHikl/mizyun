# Mizyun Privacy Policy

**Effective:** October 21, 2026 (replaces the version effective September 19, 2026)

Adam Hikl operates Mizyun and is the controller responsible for personal data described in this policy. Privacy and support requests may be sent to **support@mizyun.phiskel.com**. No public postal address is provided.

## 1. Scope

This policy covers the Mizyun mobile app (including its optional Community features), its supporting services, and the Mizyun website. The website has no sign-in or health-data form and Mizyun does not add analytics or advertising cookies to it. Hosting and security providers may process ordinary request information such as IP address, browser details, requested page, and timestamp.

## 2. Information Used by Mizyun

- Account email, authentication identifiers, and session details.
- Profile and fitness information, including height, birth year, weight, activity, goals, meals, workouts, routines, sets, runs, indoor bouldering sessions (grades, results, attempts, projects, challenge days, and optional gym names and notes), and body measurements.
- Steps. If the user enables Steps in Account & Settings, the app reads the current day's step total from Apple Health or Android Health Connect and displays it on the device. Mizyun does not upload or store those imported step totals on its server.
- Runs imported from Health. If the user opens Import runs, the app reads the user's recent running workouts and their distance from Apple Health or Android Health Connect for that screen and keeps them in memory. Only the runs the user selects and adds are saved to the user's Mizyun account (date, start time, duration, distance, and the source app and its identifier for the run). Runs that are not selected are not sent to Mizyun. Mizyun does not read routes, heart rate, or calories for this purpose, does not read in the background, and does not track later changes or deletions in the source app. Imported runs are not sent to the AI providers described in Section 4 and are not shared in Community. Disconnecting Health does not delete runs already saved; the user can delete them in the app.
- Meal photos. A photo used to estimate a meal is processed as described in Section 4. If the user chooses Keep photo with meal when saving a meal, Mizyun also stores one compressed JPEG (longest side at most 640 pixels, location and other embedded metadata removed) in private storage on Mizyun's servers, linked to that meal, and shows it as a thumbnail and larger preview. Otherwise the meal is saved without the photo.
- Other optional media, such as photos attached to a bouldering entry and, in builds where the feature is available, private progress photos, which are stored in private storage linked to the user's account.
- AI Coach conversations and memories the user chooses to save, and the text of cooking-assistant conversations that is kept only in memory on the device while the user is using it.
- Community information, if the user joins Community (optional, adults only): a handle, display name, initials badge, optional bio, follow relationships, blocks and mutes, the workouts, meals and bouldering sessions the user chooses to share, reactions, reports and appeals, and, where those features are enabled, photos the user submits for sharing, shared routines, comments, direct messages, and space (group) and challenge membership and contributions; invitations, notification settings, and records of the user's acceptance of Community terms.
- Optional product analytics, only if the user opts in (Help improve Mizyun, offered in Account & Settings, during onboarding or in an occasional reminder; it is off by default and separate from AI consent): coarse usage events such as app session starts, screens viewed, onboarding steps, the start, completion, failure stage or exit of logging flows, the kind of record created, feature action results, dashboard controls used and settings searches (result counts only), each with an event and session identifier, the platform, the app version and build, and the time. These events are stored on Mizyun's own servers and linked to the user's account. They do not include food names, health values, photos, messages or other text the user enters, and they are not sent to an analytics or advertising company.
- Support messages and feedback, consent records, and limited technical or security logs.

## 3. Purposes and Legal Bases

Mizyun processes account and fitness data to provide the service and fulfill its agreement with the user. Optional AI data and optional product analytics are processed with the user's consent, which can be withdrawn in the app. Community profiles and the items a user chooses to share are processed on the user's choice to join and to share each item, which can be withdrawn in the app by withdrawing from Community or removing the item. Health data read from Apple Health or Android Health Connect is read only after the user grants permission and starts the relevant action. Security, abuse prevention, service reliability, and support are handled for legitimate operational interests. Information may also be processed where necessary to comply with law or respond to valid legal requests.

## 4. AI Processing

AI features are optional, available only to adults (18 or older), and require the user to review and accept the in-app AI disclosure. When a user enables and uses an AI feature, the data listed for that feature is sent to the AI provider that serves it. Output is advisory and uncertain.

**Anthropic PBC (Claude)** serves Coach chat, meal estimates from typed descriptions, changes the user asks for to a meal draft, and filling in Log activity from a description (on iPhones with Apple Intelligence the app first tries to do this on the device and sends the description to Anthropic only if that does not work; on other devices it is sent to Anthropic). It receives the user's messages, recent conversation, saved Coach memories, and confirmed context from the user's profile and logs (workouts and runs, weight and measurements, meals, goals, and calorie and protein targets) for Coach; the typed meal description, the country setting and the current draft for meal estimates (descriptions that name a brand or restaurant may also be looked up on the web); and the typed activity description and the phone's current date and time for Log activity. Anthropic's commercial terms say it does not train models on this data and that it normally deletes it within 30 days, but it may keep it longer to enforce its usage policy or where the law requires.

**Google LLC (Gemini API)** serves meal and nutrition-label photo estimates (including photos sent in Coach), ingredient photos, the cooking assistant (photos, messages, the ingredients and ideas on screen, saved diet preferences, and the protein, carbohydrates and fat left for the day), meal illustrations (the meal's name, sent automatically for meals saved without a photo), and the AI-tailored evening stretch (the names and set counts of the exercises finished that day and whether the user ran or climbed). Google states that prompts, context, and output may be retained for up to **55 days** for abuse monitoring. Google's additional use of submitted data depends on the applicable service terms, billing status, and region; Mizyun does not promise that every submission is excluded from product improvement.

Users who consented under the earlier disclosure, which named only Google, have their AI features served by Google only; features are served by Anthropic only for users whose saved consent is a version that names Anthropic. The photo kept with a meal (Section 2) is stored only by Mizyun and is not sent to an AI provider unless the user submits it for estimation.

## 5. Recipients and International Transfers

Data is shared only as needed with service providers supporting authentication, hosting, storage, email, security, and optional AI. These currently include Anthropic for Claude AI processing and Google for Gemini AI processing (Section 4), Cloudflare for DNS and support-email routing, and an Amazon SES integration for email delivery. Providers may process data outside the user's country. Where data protection law requires a transfer safeguard, Mizyun relies on the applicable contractual or legal mechanism offered for that service.

Community content is not shared with these providers for advertising. Within Community, the content a user chooses to share is visible to the audience described in the sharing preview (for example approved followers, or members of a space or challenge the user joined), and other members can copy or screenshot what they see. Mizyun does not sell personal data and does not use it for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

## 6. Security and Retention

Mizyun uses encrypted transport, authenticated user-scoped access, and private media storage with time-limited access links. Active account records are kept while the account is in use and as needed to provide the service. A photo kept with a meal is kept until the user removes it, deletes the meal, or deletes the account. Product analytics events are deleted 30 days after they are recorded, and all of a user's events are deleted when the user turns analytics off. The standard retention period for operational backup archives is **90 days**; a cleanup outage may delay physical deletion. Limited records may be kept longer when required by law, to resolve a support request, or to establish or defend a legal claim. Provider-controlled copies follow the provider's applicable terms (Section 4).

Community records follow shorter limits. Shared cards stop being available at most 365 days after they are published or updated, and a card the user removes is purged within 30 days; the display name, initials and bio of a member who withdraws are purged within 30 days; notifications in the in-app inbox are kept for 90 days; a handle of a deleted account is reserved for 90 days to prevent impersonation; and reports and moderation records are kept until resolved, with the necessary evidence of a closed case kept for 180 days. Blocks and safety records may be kept for the life of the account so they continue to protect the people involved.

## 7. Choices, Export, and Deletion

In Account & Settings, users can stop Mizyun from reading steps, review or withdraw AI consent, turn product analytics on or off (turning it off deletes the user's analytics events) or clear those events, download a JSON export of available records (including analytics consent and events) with media metadata (including meal photos) and temporary download links, and start account deletion. Users can remove a photo kept with a meal at any time, withdraw from Community while keeping their private records, share or stop sharing individual items, and block, mute and report other members. Users can separately revoke Apple Health or Android Health Connect access in those apps. A successful deletion removes account data, including photos kept with meals and the user's Community profile and content (Community records are purged within 30 days of deletion unless a safety case requires a documented, time-limited hold), from active systems through a staged process. Backup copies age out under the 90-day retention policy and are not used for ordinary service operation. Files previously exported to a user's device, and copies others have already seen or saved from shared Community content, cannot be remotely erased.

Depending on location, a user may ask to access, correct, erase, restrict, or transfer personal data; object to certain processing; or withdraw consent. A user may also complain to a local data protection authority.

## 8. Children

Mizyun is not directed to children under 13, or under 16 where that is the applicable digital-consent age. Optional AI features and Community are available only to users aged 18 or older.
